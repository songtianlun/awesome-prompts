## English

**Awesome Prompts** is a curated collection of prompts for large language and multi‑modal models. The prompts are organized by modality and task type, covering text‑to‑text, text‑to‑image, and text‑to‑video scenarios.

For a richer reading experience and the full prompt library, visit [prmbr.com](https://prmbr.com/).

The visual gallery lives at [prmbr.com/cases/](https://prmbr.com/cases/). New cases are structured records in [`docs/data/cases.json`](docs/data/cases.json) (schema: [`docs/data/cases.schema.json`](docs/data/cases.schema.json)), with WebP previews in `docs/images/cases/`. Each record has a title, category, model, tags, the original prompt, a Chinese translation, usage notes, and a source URL. Older Markdown collections are unchanged and linked from the gallery as legacy collections.

This batch adds 55 verified cases: 27 from the [Google Nano Banana image docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation) (CC BY 4.0; images resized to WebP), 24 from the [OpenAI GPT Image prompting guide](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/multimodal/image-gen-models-prompting-guide) (MIT; images from the Cookbook repository, resized to WebP), and 4 from the [Sora 2 prompting guide](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/sora/sora2_prompting_guide) (prompt text from the MIT guide; result GIFs are hotlinked, not stored). Blog posts and community scrapes whose images could not be redistributed, or whose per-case prompt could not be tied to a specific result, were left out.

## 中文

**精选提示词库** 收录了面向大语言模型和多模态模型的各类提示词，并按模态与任务类型分类，包括文本生成、文生图、文生视频等情境。

为了获得更好的阅读体验和完整的提示词库，请访问 [prmbr.com](https://prmbr.com/)。

视觉案例库在 [prmbr.com/cases/](https://prmbr.com/cases/)。新增案例是 [`docs/data/cases.json`](docs/data/cases.json) 里的结构化记录（结构见 [`docs/data/cases.schema.json`](docs/data/cases.schema.json)），预览图为 `docs/images/cases/` 中的 WebP。每条包含标题、分类、模型、标签、原文提示词、中文翻译、用法提示和来源链接。原有 Markdown 合集没有改动，并在案例库里作为既有合集链接。

这一批新增 55 条可核验案例：27 条来自 [Google Nano Banana 图像文档](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/image-generation)（CC BY 4.0，图片已转为 WebP），24 条来自 [OpenAI GPT Image 提示指南](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/multimodal/image-gen-models-prompting-guide)（MIT，图片来自 Cookbook 仓库并转为 WebP），4 条来自 [Sora 2 提示指南](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/sora/sora2_prompting_guide)（提示词文本来自 MIT 指南；结果 GIF 只做外链，不入库）。博客文章和社区抓取如果图片不能再分发，或无法把提示词对应到具体结果，就没有收录。
